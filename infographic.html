Technology does more than give humans new tools. As technologies spread, they can change how we organize our lives, share knowledge, work, and make decisions.

The pattern

A technology can begin as a solution to one problem and become something much larger once people adopt it. The printing press changed the spread of knowledge. The clock changed how time could structure daily life. Computers changed our relationship with information.

So what happens when the technology starts helping us make the decisions themselves?

That is the question I want to explore next with AI.
